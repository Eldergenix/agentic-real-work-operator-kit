# Privacy, Consent, and Erasure Checklist

- [ ] Source, purpose, cloud persistence, and training consent are separate.
- [ ] Minimum-purpose projection documented.
- [ ] Raw health/sensitive values remain local unless separately authorized.
- [ ] Bitemporal timestamps and missingness codes are present.
- [ ] Explicit/user/provider facts outrank derived beliefs.
- [ ] Memory origin authority and descendants are recorded.
- [ ] Compaction creates a new write receipt and preserves origins.
- [ ] Retention/TTL and legal/operational receipt basis are declared.
- [ ] Consent withdrawal increments epoch and blocks next use/export.
- [ ] Erasure traverses raw, derived, embedding, graph, procedure, dataset, adaptation, cache, and device descendants.
- [ ] Provider deletion proof and latent/procedure rebuild proof are captured.
- [ ] User can inspect sources, recent use, corrections, and erasure status.
- [ ] Training exporter excludes erased/revoked examples.
